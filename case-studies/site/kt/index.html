Codebase guides · October 2026

Our automatic codebase guides now beat Devin's on nearly every measure.

KT docs are guides our AI writes to explain a codebase to someone new to it. We tested them against Devin's on three public codebases with 75 questions checked against the code. Better guides get new engineers productive faster.

  • 62.7%correct using only our guides (Devin: 52.3%)
  • 81.3%correct when asking our AI directly (Devin: 71.3%)
  • 2.7%of our answers made something up (Devin: 22.7%)
  • 0questions our guides could not answer (Devin: 2)

What we found

  • Before this study our guides scored 36.3%; we fixed every gap found and re-measured.
  • Our guides cover 460 source files, against 315 for Devin.
  • When our guides point to code as proof, it backs up the claim 70.8% of the time (Devin: 55.0%).
  • If Devin gets credit for answering from newer code, direct answers are about even (81.3% vs 80.3%).

Where we still fall short

  • A reader using only our guides still scores 62.7%: a strong start, not a substitute for the code.
  • Fixes were checked on the same questions that exposed the problems; a fresh codebase test is still to come.
  • Each guide now costs $4.42 and 15 minutes to write, about double before. Devin does not publish its cost.

Bottom line: Our guides are now more complete and more accurate than Devin's, and our AI makes things up far less often. Next we confirm this on an untested codebase.
